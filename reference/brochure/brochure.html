Since 2017

The journey thus far

2017
Ground is broken for our first kitchen, at Pur Heeran, Hoshiarpur
FEB 2019
Langar begins at five civil hospitals: Hoshiarpur, Dasuya, Mukerian, Batala, and Nawanshahr
APR 2019
Daily meals begin at a school and a care home
OCT 2019
Service reaches Amritsar
2020
COVID-19: up to 300,000 meals a day, cooked around the clock with the local community
OCT 2021
Our second kitchen opens at Madhir, Sri Muktsar Sahib
2025
Our free clinic opens
TODAY
32 hospitals served daily, including a new Langar hall at Sector 32, Chandigarh. About 115,000 meals a day
What comes next

The future we are building

A dedicated dairy farm. Underway: flooring is complete, and it will support 250 cows, giving pure milk and fresh ghee for Langar.
Patient-specific hospital meals. Meals prepared to each patient’s diet orders, starting with a pilot at Civil Hospital, Hoshiarpur.
Ambulances on Punjab’s major highways. Emergency cover today; next, the entire GT Road across Punjab within a couple of years.
A third kitchen at Kartarpur. Opening October 2028 on a 7-acre site with a school and hospital complex.
Groundbreaking October 2027
A world-class charitable hospital

The land is acquired. Ground breaks in October 2027, with completion by October 2034, the 500th birth anniversary of Sri Guru Ram Das Ji.
